## Driver adjustments and limits

The stock Jackal lobby driver timed out with zero shots: its idle runner
remained behind cover, giving the aim helper no visible target. The successful
run also drove the host runner with W, Space and a short D strafe to expose it.
This was a driver limitation rather than an observed game failure.

The temporary pause assertion initially expected a `paused` phase. The actual
public state keeps `phase: 'playing'` and uses `run.paused`; the corrected
assertion observed the shared pause successfully.

The Mama aim helper's generated vertical mouse delta had the opposite sign
from the running game's controls, pushing pitch to its limits. Correcting that
generated input made the helper aim correctly. The additional guest-Mama round
used the corrected sign from the start and disabled the host driver's firing,
so its elimination proves the guest's shot reached the host. Production input,
rules and networking were unchanged.

Headless Chrome cannot acquire pointer lock. The skill's helpers stand in for
`document.pointerLockElement` and then dispatch keyboard/pointer/mouse events.
Real pointer-lock acquisition, audio and gameplay feel still need human review.
